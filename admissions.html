Five Simple Steps

Our Illustrative Admission Process

A transparent, student-friendly journey designed to understand your child's potential, interests, and readiness for residential life.

1

Submit Enquiry

Fill out the online enquiry form or visit our admissions office to receive comprehensive institutional brochures and fee structures.

2

Receive Info Kit

Our admissions counsel contacts you with an informational dossier, curriculum guidelines, and scheduled campus walkthrough slots.

3

Submit Application

Complete the student profile dossier accompanied by prior academic report cards, medical declarations, and relevant identity documents.

4

Friendly Interaction

A warm, informal interactive dialogue with educators to gauge the child's curiosity, linguistic readiness, and boarding adaptability.

5

Admission Confirmation

Upon positive review, an offer letter is issued along with boarding kit guidelines, house assignment, and orientation schedule.

Age & Academic Criteria

General Eligibility Guidelines

We welcome students across Grades 4 through 12 who demonstrate intellectual curiosity, emotional maturity, and enthusiasm for community living.

Primary Boarding (Grades 4 – 5)

Generally aged 9 to 10 years. Focus is placed on emotional readiness, basic self-care, and curiosity.

Middle School (Grades 6 – 8)

Generally aged 11 to 13 years. Assessed on foundational conceptual clarity and collaborative peer attitudes.

Secondary & Senior (Grades 9 – 12)

Generally aged 14 to 17 years. Evaluated on academic track record, discipline, and stream specialization aspirations.

Application Dossier

Sample Documents Checklist

Please ensure you have digital or physical copies of the following sample records during registration:

  • Recent passport-sized photographs of student and parents/guardians
  • Previous academic years' report cards / transcripts of marks
  • Official government birth certificate (proof of date of birth)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous recognized educational institution
  • Comprehensive student medical fitness certificate & immunization records
  • Parent identity proof and residential address documentation

Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding admissions and boarding life.

While children adapt wonderfully at various ages, Grades 4 to 6 (ages 9–11) represent a particularly natural transition period where children develop independence, teamwork, and strong peer companionship easily under our nurturing junior house mothers.

Every new boarder is paired with a caring senior "buddy" from their House and receives attentive daily guidance from their House Parent. Engaging evening routines, structured sports, and warm communal dining ensure students quickly feel embraced by the school family.

Scheduled weekly telephonic or video calls are arranged during evening hours. Parents may visit during designated exeat weekends and campus festivals, ensuring healthy family ties alongside undisturbed boarding routine focus.

Our dining facility prepares wholesome, hygienically supervised vegetarian meals with Jain options, fresh seasonal fruits, milk, and balanced nutrition planned by certified dietitians. Any medical dietary restrictions are carefully catalogued by our health centre.
