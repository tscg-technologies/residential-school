Welcome Note

More Than a School. A Place to Belong.

At Residential School, we believe that real education extends seamlessly beyond textbooks and standard lecture periods into round-the-clock habits of mind, empathy, and collaborative character.

Set amidst tranquil grounds thoughtfully designed for scholarly focus and wholesome outdoor life, Residential School offers a sanctuary where students grow under the round-the-clock mentorship of passionate educators and dedicated pastoral house masters.

From morning physical conditioning and vibrant scientific discovery sessions to shared evening dining and quiet prep study hours, our students cultivate personal independence, enduring values, and emotional resilience.

Residential Experience

A Home Away From Home

Residential life at Residential School is designed to foster genuine camaraderie, daily discipline, self-reliance, and lifelong fraternal bonds. Under the guidance of experienced house masters, every child feels valued, heard, and supported.

  • Comfortable Boarding Rooms: Well-ventilated spaces with ergonomic study tables and personal storage.
  • Supervised Daily Routines: Structured balances of rigorous study, physical exercise, and creative leisure.
  • Nutritious Dining: Wholesome, hygienically prepared multi-cuisine meals crafted by certified nutritionists.
  • Pastoral Care & Support: Round-the-clock infirmary, clinical counselors, and dedicated house mothers.

A Disciplined Rhythm

Daily Life at Residential School

A harmonious daily schedule ensuring academic concentration, physical vitality, reflection, and joyful companionship.

6:00 AM

Morning Routine & Awakening

Rising bell, personal hygiene, bed-making, and room inspection under pastoral house mentor oversight.

6:30 AM

Fitness & Outdoor Activities

Energizing morning jog, yoga asanas, aerobic conditioning, and outdoor drill on campus grounds.

7:30 AM

Nutritious Breakfast

Warm, wholesome, dietitian-balanced breakfast in the central dining hall fostering community fellowship.

8:30 AM

Academic Sessions & Laboratory Work

Morning assembly, value reflections, core academic periods, lab experiments, and collaborative seminars.

1:00 PM

Lunch & Midday Rest

Hot communal lunch followed by relaxation, quiet reading, and recreation in house common rooms.

2:00 PM

Learning, Clubs & Tutorials

Afternoon academic sessions, remedial tutorial assistance, club societies, and library research.

4:00 PM

Sports & Outdoor Recreation

Field sports, competitive house leagues, martial arts, swimming, and refreshing evening snacks.

6:00 PM

Supervised Evening Study & Reading

Dedicated quiet prep time in study halls with resident faculty assisting students with homework and projects.

7:30 PM

Community Dinner & House Fellowship

Evenings meal together, informal dorm interactions, house meetings, indoor games, and phone calls home.

9:00 PM

Night Reflection & Lights Out

Personal diary entries, calming wind-down, mentor check-ins, and restful sleep for a recharged tomorrow.

* Illustrative schedule timings subject to seasonal adjustments, house events, and special academic examinations.
