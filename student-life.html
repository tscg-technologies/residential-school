Student Life & Co-Curriculars

Fostering diverse passions, teamwork, artistic brilliance, athletic tenacity, and confident character beyond the classroom.

A Rich Co-Curricular Canvas

Where Passions Flourish & Leaders Emerge

At Residential School, an education is incomplete if it does not nurture the soul through music, sharpen the reflexes through athletics, test convictions through public discourse, and awaken empathy through community service.

Our boarding environment affords young people the extraordinary luxury of uninterrupted time: afternoon sporting leagues on manicured lawns, twilight acoustic concerts on the amphitheater steps, and late-evening debates in the heritage reading rooms.

Whether a student dreams of captaining an athletic squad, directing a Shakespearean drama, coding autonomous robotics solutions, or publishing an original literary magazine, our faculty coaches provide the platform, equipment, and encouragement to transform curiosity into mastery.

Creative Energy Students discovering self-expression through fine arts, theater, and music.
Spheres of Engagement

Diverse Avenues for Wholesome Development

A balanced array of sporting disciplines, fine arts, intellectual societies, and leadership avenues.

Sports & Athletics

Field sports build physical resilience, grace in victory, and dignity in defeat. We offer comprehensive coaching in football, cricket, track-and-field athletics, lawn tennis, badminton, and martial arts.

  • • Daily evening compulsory physical conditioning
  • • Inter-House tournaments and seasonal championship cups
  • • Certified NIS coaches and fitness conditioning mentors

Music & Performing Arts

Immersing students in rich musical traditions spanning Indian classical vocal, sitar, tabla, western choir ensembles, acoustic guitar, violin, and dramatic theater arts.

  • • Annual School Musical and classical music soirees
  • • Acoustic practice rooms with recording capabilities
  • • Classical Kathak, Bharatanatyam, and folk choreography

Visual Arts & Craftsmanship

Channeling creative impulses into oil painting, watercolours, ceramic pottery, woodcraft, architectural sketching, and modern digital photography.

  • • Annual Campus Art Exhibition and charity auctions
  • • Professional potter's wheels and ceramic kiln firing
  • • Printmaking, calligraphy, and sculpture ateliers

Clubs & Societies

Student-run forums fostering focused intellectual passions: Robotics and Coding, Parliamentary Debate, Model United Nations, Astronomy, Eco-Club, and Philosophy.

  • • Weekly club meetings led by elected student secretaries
  • • Participation in national invitational debates and hackathons
  • • Stargazing nights with high-magnification telescopes

Leadership & Governance

Instilling democratic civic consciousness through our Student Prefectorial Council, House Captaincies, Dining Committee, and Editorial Boards.

  • • Formal Investiture Ceremony every academic term
  • • Student representatives voice peer feedback to faculty
  • • Mentorship duties assigned to senior boarders for juniors

Cultural Celebrations

Celebrating the rich multicultural heritage of India: Diwali illuminations, Eid festivities, Christmas carols, Holi colors, and national holidays commemorated with unity.

  • • Traditional feasts and thematic evening dinners
  • • Student-curated folk presentations and rangoli competitions
  • • Fostering reverence and harmony across all traditions
Empathy & Service

Community Outreach & Social Service

Leadership finds its highest realization in humble service. Every residential student participates in civic action initiatives that touch neighboring communities, foster social awareness, and inspire enduring humility.

  • Peer Literacy Drives: Weekend reading and numerical tutoring for children from surrounding regional villages.
  • Campus Afforestation: Native tree sapling plantations, composting, and solar conservation monitoring.
  • Elder Care Visits: Periodic visits to elder care homes for musical performances, shared meals, and companionship.
