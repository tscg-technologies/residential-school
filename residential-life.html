The Boarding Experience

Where Community Shapes Character

Living together in a vibrant educational sanctuary teaches young people lessons that cannot be found in conventional textbooks: how to compromise with grace, celebrate a peer's victory, maintain personal spaces with pride, and organize time with maturity.

At Residential School, our boarding houses are not mere sleeping quarters—they are lively, warm, and supportive communities governed by trust, mutual care, and the affectionate supervision of experienced resident House Parents.

Here, every student is surrounded by peers who motivate their studies, coaches who cultivate their sporting talents, and pastoral counselors who listen to their aspirations with patience and compassion.

Living Spaces

Boarding Accommodation & Comfort

Purpose-built residential wings offering safety, privacy, personal organization, and cheerful companionship.

Ergonomic Living Quarters

Bright, well-ventilated rooms with sturdy single beds, orthopedic mattresses, personal wardrobes, and dedicated study desks fitted with individual reading lamps.

Common Recreation Lounges

Each boarding wing features a relaxed common room equipped with indoor games (chess, table tennis, carrom), newspapers, and curated fiction books for evening recreation.

24/7 Safety & House Tutors

Round-the-clock security personnel, electronic biometric access gates to residence wings, and resident house masters residing on every floor ensure paramount student safety.

Fraternal Tradition

Every student belongs to one of four historic Houses, fostering lifelong loyalty, healthy athletic competition, and collaborative leadership.

Cedar House

Health & Sustenance

Nutritious Dining & Healthful Living

Physical and intellectual vigor requires wholesome, balanced, and hygienic sustenance.

Our grand central Dining Hall serves four fresh meals daily—breakfast, lunch, afternoon refreshments, and dinner. The menu is scientifically curated by certified pediatric dietitians to ensure adequate protein, complex carbohydrates, essential micronutrients, and fresh greens tailored for growing youths.

  • ✓ Pure Vegetarian & Balanced Options: Prepared in state-of-the-art stainless steel kitchens under rigid hygienic standards.
  • ✓ Filtered RO Drinking Water: Central water treatment facility ensuring mineral-balanced, tested potable water throughout campus.
  • ✓ Communal Table Manners: Faculty sit with students at communal tables, reinforcing gracious table etiquette, conversation, and solidarity.

Emotional & Physical Wellbeing

Pastoral Care, Guidance & Healthcare

A multi-layered circle of care ensuring every child feels safe, emotionally supported, and physically protected.

Pastoral House Mentors

Resident house parents act as local guardians, celebrating birthdays, noticing changes in mood, encouraging good study habits, and providing a comforting presence during moments of homesickness.

24/7 Campus Health Centre

A modern 8-bed medical infirmary with registered resident nursing staff, essential pharmaceuticals, daily visiting pediatric doctors, and standing ambulance tie-ups with regional multi-specialty hospitals.

Counselling & Parent Sync

Qualified adolescent psychological counselors provide confidential guidance for emotional adjustments, alongside regular scheduled video sync calls for parents.
